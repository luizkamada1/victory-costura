Scope: the whole app shell and every working screen (Apontamento, Eficiência, Tickets, Costureiras, Operações, Itens, Jornada). Visitor mode: Operate. Code-led (Operate surface).

Audience and task: the sewing lead scans the day's coupons on a factory PC under daylight/fluorescent light; management registers the base data and reads efficiency per period. Pinned by the user: collapsible side menu; every table sortable and filterable; Operações split into two tables (Parte Superior, Parte Inferior).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The plant's own cloth sets the screen. Refuses the grey SaaS admin with one indigo accent and stat cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pijama navy rail with a faint canelado rib, cotton-white work ground, stripe blue for selection and focus, navy ink for primary actions; hairline rules instead of shadowed cards; tabular figures; lucide line icons.

STORY: The lead sees whose day it is, scans, and watches the day fill rib by rib; management compares people on one honest scale.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail (brand crown, seven destinations grouped Dia a dia / Cadastros, collapse control). Content: page title, then the scan bar (seamstress, date, code field) as the dominant control, then the jornada strip, then the day's coupons table.
